A letter written beneath the same sky

To My Lady,
Sofia ♡

From two people who met by chance online, to two hearts that found something neither of us expected.

Chapter I · Where it began

Two strangers.

Sometimes the biggest chapters of our lives begin with something so ordinary that we don't realize its importance at the time.

We were simply two people who happened to find each other's accounts online. A follow. A message. A conversation. Nothing that looked extraordinary from the outside.

But then I spoke to you for the first time, Sofia, and something changed inside me.

From the very first day I spoke to you, I couldn't stop thinking about you. I really couldn't.

I wondered who you were, what made you laugh, what you cared about, what dreams lived inside your mind, and what kind of person existed beneath everything the world could see.

At first, I didn't have a perfect explanation for what I was feeling. I simply knew that talking to you made ordinary days feel different. I started looking forward to our conversations. A notification from you could turn a quiet moment into something I wanted to remember.

✦ ♡ ✦

Friendship became familiarity. Familiarity became trust. Trust became genuine care. And somewhere along the way, what began as two people meeting randomly online became something much more meaningful.

Chapter II · Your confession

You were brave enough to say it.

Sofia, there is one message from you that I never want to forget. Not because it was perfect, but because it was honest.

"Alright i think I'll go crazy n sm such if i dont man up and just say it. I know you have no interest in me but i feel like i should get this off my chest n all. Im not going to regret what im about to say, im not going to feel embarrassed or sad i just feel like i should tell you because i feel comfortable n all saying this. I like you shaun and you should tell yourself everyday how likable and amazing you are in general. So yea i just thought id say that"

You probably didn't realize how much courage there was in those words.

You didn't try to make the moment complicated. You simply decided to be honest, because you felt comfortable enough to tell me what was in your heart.

And somehow, that honesty became one of the moments that changed everything.

Chapter III · Why you

Why I love you ♡

I didn't fall for some perfect image of you. I fell for the person I gradually discovered.

Your heart.

The way you can care, even when you have your own things happening, is something I treasure.

Your mind.

Your thoughts, curiosity, intelligence and the way you see the world make me want to keep discovering who you are.

Your humour.

The little moments that make us laugh can mean more than grand gestures ever could.

Your dreams.

I love that you have a future ahead of you that is yours to build, with your own hopes, goals and adventures.

Your imperfections.

You don't need to be flawless to be precious. Being human, learning and growing are part of what makes a person real.

Simply being you.

There isn't one single feature I can point to and say, “That's the reason.” It is the whole person I came to know.

You remind me of a lily: gentle without being weak, beautiful without needing to prove it, and capable of bringing softness into a place simply by being there.

Chapter IV · The little things

Even ordinary things became memories.

One of the strangest and most beautiful things about caring deeply for someone is that the world doesn't actually change — but somehow, you experience it differently.

I can look at the stars and wonder whether you're somewhere beneath that same sky.

I can see a sunset painting the horizon in gold, pink and violet, and suddenly think of you.

I can see a flower blooming, hear a song, notice the moon, watch the rain, walk through nature, or experience something completely ordinary — and somehow a little part of my mind says, “Sofia would love this.”

That's what you became to me. Not just someone I talk to, but someone whose presence somehow found its way into the way I notice the world.

☾ ✦ ♡ ✦ ☼

Maybe that's why distance feels so strange. The kilometres are real, but they can't measure memories. They can't measure trust. They can't measure how much someone can mean to another person.

Chapter V · Love has many metaphors

Across different worlds.

The bird and the fish.

A bird belongs to the sky. A fish belongs to the sea. Their worlds are different, and neither can simply become the other. Yet the story reminds us of something important: loving someone doesn't mean forcing them into your world. It means learning to appreciate theirs.

The moon and the ocean.

The moon doesn't touch the ocean with hands, yet its presence can influence the tides. Sometimes someone can be far away and still have a profound place in your thoughts and heart.

The lighthouse and the ship.

A lighthouse doesn't chase a ship across the sea. It simply keeps shining, offering a point of light through the darkness. That's how I want my care for you to be: never controlling, never demanding — simply a light that wishes you well.

The lily after rain.

A flower doesn't rush its blooming. It grows through changing weather, sunlight and rain. Love can be like that too: something that needs patience, care, room to breathe and time to grow.

Real love isn't proven by how tightly we hold someone. It is shown by how respectfully we care for them, how patiently we listen, and how genuinely we want them to be happy.

Chapter VI · What I promise to learn

Love is more than beautiful words.

I want to love you in a way that is mature, respectful and responsible.

That means listening when you speak. It means respecting your boundaries. It means understanding that you have your own life, responsibilities, dreams, friends, family, school and future.

It means celebrating your happiness rather than making everything about me. It means being patient when life becomes difficult. It means apologizing when I'm wrong and learning from my mistakes.

And when someone treats you badly, I want to be someone who supports you and stands beside you — not someone who creates another problem for you. Your safety, dignity and peace matter more than proving anything.

My Lady, My Princess, My Baby, My Honey, My Darling, My everything — those names are little expressions of affection. They don't define you, limit you, or take away your independence. You are Sofia first: your own person, with your own voice and your own future.

Chapter VII · If one day you forget

If you ever forget me...

There is something I want to say with all the respect I can give you.

If one day life takes us down different roads, and somehow you forget me, I would never want that to become a reason for me to disturb your life.

I would still care about your happiness. I would still hope that you're safe, that you're smiling, that you're surrounded by people who treat you with kindness, and that you become everything you dream of becoming.

When I say I would “watch over you,” I don't mean following you or intruding into your life. I mean that somewhere in my heart, I would still wish you well. I would carry the memory of what you meant to me without demanding anything from you.

Because real love should never become possession.

If loving you ever means letting you walk freely toward the life that makes you happy, then I want to be mature enough to understand that.

♡

But while our paths are together, I want to appreciate every chapter instead of worrying about the last page.

Chapter VIII · A future I can imagine

Seven to nine years from now.

Neither of us can see the future perfectly. We're still growing. We're still learning. Life will change us in ways we can't predict.

But if, seven to nine years from now, our paths have continued together, if we have both grown into people who still choose one another, if our dreams can fit beside each other, and if the love we have today has matured into something even stronger...

Then, My Lady, I want you to know that I can imagine myself standing before you with a ring and asking you to marry me.

Not because a promise made today can control tomorrow, but because I hope that one day, when we're older and ready, we can look back at where everything started and smile at how two people who met randomly online somehow found their way to each other.

And if that day ever comes, I would be honoured to say:

“Sofia Shaw, will you marry me?”

And perhaps, one beautiful day, the name will be:

Sofia Babao. ♡

The last page, for now

My Lady. My Princess.

My Baby. My Honey. My Darling. My everything.

From the first message to every conversation, every laugh, every difficult moment, every apology, every little memory, and every ordinary moment that somehow became special because it reminded me of you — thank you.

Thank you for being brave enough to tell me how you felt. Thank you for trusting me with pieces of yourself. Thank you for becoming someone I could care about so deeply.

I don't know every road ahead of us. But I know this:

You are special to me, Sofia.

And no matter how many kilometres exist between two places, no map can measure what a person means to you.

So whenever you look at the sunset, the stars, a lily, the moon, or the quiet beauty of nature, I hope you remember that somewhere beneath this enormous sky is someone who once looked at those same things and thought of you.

✦ ♡ ✦

I love you, Sofia. ♡

Whatever the future writes, I'm grateful that our story began.

— Shaun ♡
